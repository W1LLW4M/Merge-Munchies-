# 🌈 Emoji RNG

**Roll emojis. Discover rarities. Build your collection. Become an RNG legend!**

Emoji RNG is a colourful, animated emoji-collecting simulator inspired by RNG games. Roll for emojis, discover rare collectibles, earn coins, upgrade your luck, and see how far your collection can go!

## ✨ Features

* 🎲 **Emoji Rolling** — Roll for emojis across multiple rarity tiers.
* 🌈 **167 Emoji Collectibles** — Discover emojis with unique names, shortcodes, groups, and mastery progression.
* 💎 **Rare Discoveries** — Find Legendary, Mythic, Divine, Celestial, Secret, and other rare emojis.
* 🎬 **Special Roll Animations** — Enjoy dramatic reveals and effects for high-rarity discoveries.
* 🎡 **Rainbow Prize Wheel** — Spin for coins, Prism Shards, free rolls, and temporary boosts.
* 🪙 **Coins & Passive Income** — Earn currency and progress through the game.
* 🍀 **Luck Upgrades** — Improve your chances of finding rare emojis.
* ⚡ **Speed Upgrades** — Make ordinary rolls faster.
* 🔁 **Auto-Roll** — Keep rolling automatically.
* 📚 **Collection & Favourites** — Browse, filter, collect, and equip emojis.
* 🧪 **Emoji Workshop** — Craft emojis, earn Prism Shards, and activate special boosts.
* 🎁 **Daily Rewards & Quests** — Return for rewards and complete missions.
* 🏆 **Achievements & Mastery** — Track your progress and improve your collection.
* 🌟 **Rainbow Rebirth** — Reset ordinary upgrades for permanent bonuses.
* 💾 **Local Autosave** — Save your progress in your browser.
* 🎨 **Colourful UI & Animations** — Enjoy rainbow effects, particles, glowing cards, and animated reveals.
* 🖥️ **Fullscreen Mode** — Play with a more immersive interface.

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the HTML game file in your browser.
3. Click **Roll** to discover your first emojis.
4. Collect emojis and earn coins.
5. Equip emojis and purchase upgrades.
6. Spin the Rainbow Wheel for extra rewards.
7. Complete quests, unlock achievements, and discover the rarest emojis!

## 💻 Run Locally

Emoji RNG is designed to run as a browser-based HTML game.

No installer or backend server is required for basic local play.

## 💾 Save Data

Game progress is saved locally in your browser. Clearing browser data or switching browsers may affect your save, so export a backup if that option is available in your version.
